<objective>
Give the MSI the same end state as the Inno installer: an installed app and a registered, auto-starting service
under `NT AUTHORITY\LocalService` with the recovery actions.

Purpose: MSI-03, and D-12-04. Parity here means the same state afterwards, not the same bytes in the script.
</objective>

<tasks>

1. Add two type-34 custom actions (`Directory` + `ExeCommand`) invoking the installed `Synapic.Indexer.exe`
   with `--install` and `--uninstall`; `Execute="deferred"`, `Impersonate="no"`.
2. Sequence them: install after `InstallFiles` under `NOT Installed`; uninstall before `RemoveFiles` under
   `REMOVE~="ALL"`.
3. Document the `Return` choices: check on install (rollback over a service-less install), ignore on uninstall.

</tasks>
